Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
An introductory course for undergraduates. It covers agents, search, games, knowledge representation, reasoning under uncertainty, basics of machine learning, and image processing. It closes with an overview of reinforcement learning. Machine Learning
A course for senior undergraduates and beginning graduate students. It covers the core supervised models from linear regression to neural networks. It also covers model evaluation and unsupervised learning. I teach it in an introductory form and in a more advanced statistical form.
