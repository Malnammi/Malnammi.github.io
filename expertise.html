Areas of Expertise

My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning, scalable research infrastructure, workflow automation, and agentic AI systems.

  • Applied machine learning. Problem framing, model selection, and evaluation for scientific and industrial problems in chemistry, manufacturing, energy, and logistics.
  • Scalable ML pipelines. Distributed computation, large batch experiment design, and reproducible research infrastructure.
  • Workflow automation. Reliable automated workflows that turn multi-day manual tasks into single repeatable runs. The automation behaves the same from run to run, and its results are reproducible and auditable.
  • Agentic AI workflows. AI agents that speed up software development and research while every output stays reviewed and traceable. Suited to high-stakes work where errors are costly.
  • AI-assisted development practice. Adoption patterns with appropriate verification practices.

Tools and Methods

  • Machine learning and data science. Python (PyTorch, PyTorch Forecasting, TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, Gym), MATLAB, and R. Reinforcement learning, active learning, graph neural networks and transformers, time-series forecasting, and model selection and evaluation at scale. Working knowledge of experiment tracking with Weights & Biases.
  • Agentic AI and AI-augmented research. Agentic coding tools applied to software development, experiment management, analysis and reporting, and manuscript preparation. A person reviews agent-produced work before it ships, including peer-reviewed scientific code where results cannot simply be rerun.
